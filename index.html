Dhanika Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · applies to the Dhanika Android app (com.dhanika.app)

Summary

Dhanika is an offline expense tracker. It does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The income, savings, RD, investment, EPF and expense records and the settings you enter are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Only Dhanika can read them.

What the app does not do

  • It has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
  • It does not read your SMS, contacts, location, photos or bank details.
  • Your data is not included in Android cloud backups.

Backups you create

If you use Back up to a file or Export as spreadsheet, Dhanika writes a file to the location you choose. That file is then under your control. If you save it to a cloud folder or share it, that service's privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the app. Either one permanently deletes everything Dhanika has stored. Neither we nor anyone else has a copy.

Children

Dhanika is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
